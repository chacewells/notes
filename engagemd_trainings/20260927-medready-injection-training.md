# Lupron - suppress hormone cycle

- stimulates pituitary gland
- suppresses cycle
- check dose & timeframe

## Injection protocol

- subcutaneous
- 14-day kit - syringes & alcohol pads
- 1 needle per use
- vial can be used until exhausted

### First time use

- remove covering
- sanitize before using
- fill syringe with as much air as dosage
- inject in vial
- draw dosage out w/ plunger
- don't let bubbles in
- clean injection site with alcohol
- inject w/in 3 in radius of belly button

# Delestrogen

- estrogen medication
- delivered in oil
- matures uteran lining for embryo transfer
- intramuscular - deep into muscle

## Supplies

- Multi-dose Delestrogen vial
- syringe for drawing up
- injection syringe
- alcohol pads
- vials container

## First time use

- should be clear & yellow color
- remove cap & sanitize

## Usage

- sanitize
- attach needle to syringe
- draw back as much air as dosage, fill vial with air
- draw dosage (needle under liquid line)
- separate injection needle, cover drawing needle, replace with injection needle

# Progesterone

- intramuscular
- don't reuse needles/syringes
- doses different from own

## Supplies

- vial, syringes, draw syringe & injection syringe

## Procedure

- liquid should be clear & yellow
- sanitize
- fill air to dosage level
- insert needle, send air into vial
- draw medicine, needle below liquid level
- replace draw needle w/injection needle
- dispose needle in sharps container

# Subcutaneous injection

- select injection site, alternate per injection
- belly below button, or upper-outer thigh
- pinch, inject, release skin during injection

# Intramuscular injection

## Injection sites

- upper-outer buttocks
- center of front thigh
- alternate sites

## Procedure

- clean site w/ alcohol
- spread skin, hold taught
- insert entire length of needle quickly
- pull back slightly to check for blood; dispose if blood appears
- no blood - should see air
- slowly push plunger in completely
- dispose needle & syringe in sharps container
