# Menstrual Cycle

- regulated by coordinated hormonal changes
- hypothalamus - GNRH - stimulates pituitary gland
- pituitary gland triggers: FSH (follicle stimulating hormone), Luteneizing Hormone (LH)

## Phases

### Follicular - single follical stimulated over 14 days - more estrogen

- thickens uternan lining - endometrium
- LH completes maturation of egg, triggers ovulation

## Luteal

- egg not fertilized, corpus luteum denegerates after 14 days
- uteran lining thins
- if fertilized, implants in uterus
- embryo produces hCG: stimulates corpus luteum to produce more progesterone, preventing loss of uteran lining

# FET cycle

- goal: mimic optimal menstrual cycle
- pre-treatment: suppress hormones to time cycle - birth control pill

## Baseline status

- test for readiness

## Beginning of cycle

- estrogen: facilitate growth of endometrium
- ultrasound & bloodwork: test endometrial lining & success of estrogen treatment
- progesterone: added to protocol (support uteran lining)
- unexpected ovulation may occur: < 3% of cases

## Frozen embryo transfer

- day of implantation: embryo thawed
- if embryo doesn't survive, another embryo thawed
- come in w/ moderately full bladder
- embryo tranferred w/ cathater

## Number of embryos to transfer

- avoiding multiple pregnancy: best approach transfer only 1 embryo
- chromosome abnormalities - advanced age
- egg from patient <35yo - singleton pregnancy likely
- multiple transfers - 1 in 4 multiple pregnancy rate
- embryo can split - identical twins
